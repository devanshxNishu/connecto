# Code of conduct

Connecto is a small project, and everyone who takes part - in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the
project lives - helps keep it a friendly place to work.

**Please do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work rather than the person, and credit
other people's ideas.

**Please do not:** harass, insult or threaten anyone, make sexual or discriminatory remarks, publish someone's private
information, or carry on after someone has asked you to stop.

**If something goes wrong:** tell the maintainer, [@devanshxNishu](https://github.com/devanshxNishu), privately through
the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are read by the maintainer only and handled discreetly. Depending on
what happened, the maintainer may ask someone to change their behaviour, remove comments or code, or block someone from
the project.

Security problems have their own, private route: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
